Description

Two vintage **Gilhe's Gentlemen's Attire** ("At The Square") menswear catalogs from the early 1980s — an obscure but well-appointed regional clothier out of Baton Rouge, Louisiana. A nice pair of trad/Ivy-era menswear ephemera.

What's Included

  • **Catalog A:** ornate embossed maroon cover (Victorian-style scrollwork border) with the "Gilhe's Gentlemen's Attire" crest.
  • **Catalog B:** photographic cover (a couple in tweeds at a horse farm) with the same logo.
  • 2 catalogs total, sold together as a lot.

Condition

Honest vintage wear; both complete-looking and readable.

  • Cover A: edge/corner wear, creasing, light surface rubbing.
  • Cover B: handling and edge wear.
  • Interiors shown: clean and bright; bindings intact.
  • **These are original mailed copies:** each back cover carries its original bulk-rate mailing label (the addressee is blocked out for privacy in the listing photos).
  • Not assessed: exact page counts, spine tightness, unshown interior pages.

About this item

A time capsule of early-'80s traditional American menswear — the catalogs feature Norman Hilton country suiting, Alden tassel loafers, and Merona sportswear, styled in the classic Ivy/preppy idiom. A fun reference pair for trad-menswear collectors or Baton Rouge local-history buffs.
