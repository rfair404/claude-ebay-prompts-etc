## 3. Your promises

You promise that:
(a) you own the items, or have the authority to sell them (see Exhibit B if you sign for a trust, estate, another person or a company);
(b) the items are free of any lien, loan or claim;
(c) they are not stolen; and
(d) you will defend our title to the items against anyone else's claim.

## 4. As-is

We are buying the items as-is, where-is. You make no promise about their condition. Your promises in section 3 are about ownership only.

## 5. Title and delivery

5.1. Ownership (title) passes to us when you receive payment.

5.2. You hand over the items when we pay, unless we agree on another date here: __________.

## 6. Signing for someone else

If you sign for a trust, estate, another person or a company, you also sign Exhibit B (certification of authority) and attach the proof it lists.

## 7. General

7.1. Georgia law governs this agreement.

7.2. This agreement and its exhibits are our whole deal. Changes must be in writing and agreed by both of us.

7.3. You get a copy of everything you sign.
