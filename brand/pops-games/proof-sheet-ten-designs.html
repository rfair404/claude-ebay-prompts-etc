Printing notes

  • Sheet is 12-up on US Letter — three across, four down, 2″ squares with a ⅕″ gutter and a dotted cut guide on every card.
  • In the print dialog set scale to 100% (not “fit to page”) or the squares come out under 2″, and turn on background graphics or the ink drops out entirely.
  • Cardstock at 65–110 lb cover holds the flat color best. A paper trimmer beats scissors across a dotted guide.
  • Cards 04, 07 and 08 lay down a lot of navy and red — if your printer struggles with heavy coverage, 05 and 10 use the least ink by a wide margin.
