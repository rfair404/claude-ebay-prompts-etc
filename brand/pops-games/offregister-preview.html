How it prints

  • 20 per sheet — 4 across × 5 down, no gutters. Cards share edges, so trimming is 3 vertical cuts and 4 horizontal, plus the outer border.
  • Scale must be 100%. “Fit to page” will shrink the squares under 2″ and throw the cut spacing off.
  • Background graphics on — the red plate is a background color on the ghost layer and will drop out otherwise.
  • White is bare paper here, so pick the stock you want the card to be. 65–80 lb cover feeds through most color lasers without curling.
  • Hairline guides print at 0.25 pt light grey. If your trimmer has a good scale, switch to corner ticks and the cards come out with nothing on them at all.
