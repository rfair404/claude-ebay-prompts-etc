─── METHODOLOGICAL NOTE: Tier 2 validation ───

This is the first manual test of the tiered search strategy. The
result validates the design:
  - Tier 1 (WebSearch) failed to find an exact-match comp for a
    niche store-edition catalog — confirmed expected limitation.
  - Tier 2 (Claude in Chrome → eBay sold-listings) found a clean
    exact-match Tier A comp on the first search.
  - The escalation cost was modest (one Chrome navigation + one
    page-text extraction) and the output is substantially
    more valuable than what Tier 1 alone produced.

This is the kind of behavior the tiered approach was designed for
— skip the expensive Tier 2 call when Tier 1 succeeds, but reach
for it whenever an exact-match is the goal and Tier 1 misses.
Production Apify backend would replicate this Tier 2 result with
faster, more reliable, and bot-challenge-immune execution.
