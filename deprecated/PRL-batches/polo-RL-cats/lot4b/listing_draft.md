## DESCRIPTION

```
Polo Ralph Lauren × Rich's Polo Place — Fall 1987. A store mailer catalog mailed to 
Rich's customers across Atlanta in September 1987, advertising the Fall collection at 
Rich's Polo Place locations including Lenox Square, Town Center, North DeKalb, Cumberland, 
Gwinnett Place, Southlake, Perimeter, and Downtown.

The cover image — a smiling man with a gray weimaraner — and the back panel's canoe 
scene with the same dog make this one of the more visually distinctive pieces in the 
Rich's × Polo series.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
• One Polo Ralph Lauren × Rich's Polo Place fashion mailer catalog
• Fall 1987 (catalog code 9-87-12-45-A = September 1987 mailing confirmed)
• © 1987 Polo Ralph Lauren Corporation (noted on back)
• Front cover: man with gray weimaraner, green Polo Ralph Lauren logo
• Back: Rich's, 45 Broad Street SW, Atlanta, GA 30303 + bulk rate US postage paid Rich's 
  + canoe and weimaraner lifestyle photo panel + all Rich's Polo Place location listing
• Interior: men's fall collection — alpine ski editorial, Tyrolean knitwear sweaters, 
  expedition outerwear, plaid, rugby team editorial

CONDITION — HONEST DISCLOSURE
A 1987 mailer — nearly 40 years old — in honest, livable vintage condition. 
No piece is perfect, but this one remains highly collectible.

• Cover: light surface wear consistent with age; imagery bright and clean
• Back: intact mailing panel with Rich's address and postal stamp fully legible; 
  catalog code and © date clearly readable
• Interior: clean pages, no tears or writing noted

A distinctive piece for Polo RL collectors, Rich's Atlanta ephemera collectors, 
or anyone who appreciates great dog photography from the height of prep culture.
```
