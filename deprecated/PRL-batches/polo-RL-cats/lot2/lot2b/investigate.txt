## Brief summary

This is a Polo by Ralph Lauren illustrated fashion catalog, Volume II, Fall 1977 —
confirmed directly from legible text on the back cover. It is the second-ever numbered
Polo Ralph Lauren catalog, carrying the early rectangular "Polo by Ralph Lauren" box
logo that predates the later standardized "Polo Ralph Lauren" branding. The front cover
features a winter equestrian panorama co-branded with a cursive script reading "Muse's"
(Muse's Men's Store, Atlanta) — the same store co-brand seen on lot2a but more legibly
rendered here. The interior spread is a collage-style 1970s fashion editorial with polo
club lifestyle imagery including a visible Royal Palm Polo Club scoreboard. Not a mailer.
An exact-era comp sold at $214 in May 2026 for the same Volume II / Fall 1977 catalog.

- The "Polo by Ralph Lauren" box logo is a confirmed early format — distinct from
