## DESCRIPTION (paste as-is into eBay)

Four vintage Polo Ralph Lauren catalogs from The Polo Ralph Lauren Shop in Atlanta — Park Place and Lenox Square locations. Anchored by one of the most collectible pieces in the Ralph Lauren ephemera world.

---

**Polo Ralph Lauren "On Safari"**

The crown jewel of this lot. Zebra-print burlap fabric cover with cream label bearing the cursive "On Safari" title. The editorial inside reads like a private travel diary — named models on what appears to be an actual African safari: Jane, Justin, Clotilde, Peter. Handwritten captions. Lion cubs. Safari tents at dusk. Fashion as pure adventure storytelling. "There was a profound stillness that pervaded the African landscape, that added to its immensity and greatness." One of the most desirable Polo Ralph Lauren pieces, period. Comp sales of this specific title routinely reach $300 solo.

---

**Catalog — Fall 1977 Volume II**

White cover with Polo player logo. Interior opens with a Ralph Lauren portrait spread and the definitive statement of his philosophy: "Ralph Lauren captures the essence of the American lifestyle. He expresses it with originality, but always with integrity and a respect for tradition." Editorial photography throughout. Tagline on interior: "Clothing that endures and romanticizes. That will be worn and cherished forever." This is early-era Polo at its most aspirational.

---

**Catalog — Fall 1981 Volume IV**

Dark navy cover with gold polo player. Wide-format design. Atlanta store edition.

---

**Catalog — Fall 1982**

Dark cover with red polo player logo. Atlanta store edition.

All four pieces originate from the same Atlanta Polo Ralph Lauren Shop — specific retailer provenance (Park Place 396-1800 / Lenox Square 261-2663) that adds a layer of Southern fashion history for regional collectors.

---

No piece is perfect — but this set is still desirable. The On Safari cover shows minor age toning at one edge consistent with the era. The other three catalogs present well with typical light surface wear. All four are complete with no missing pages noted.
