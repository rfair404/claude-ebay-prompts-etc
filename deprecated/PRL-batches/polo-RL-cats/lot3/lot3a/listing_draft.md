## DESCRIPTION (paste as-is into eBay)

Polo Ralph Lauren — Fall 1988, Volume VI. Official in-store catalog for The Polo Ralph Lauren Atlanta Shop at Lenox Square and Park Place Shopping Center.

**Cover:** Warm editorial still life — flowers, a framed daguerreotype portrait, antique objects arranged on a surface. Gold serif "POLO RALPH LAUREN FALL 1988" across the top.

**Interior:** Suits, cashmere knitwear, expedition outerwear with coyote-trimmed hoods, Tyrolean knitwear. Alpine and coastal editorial photography. Original mail-order form intact inside the back.

**Back cover:** Cream ground. "Vol VI Fall 1988." Atlanta retail locations: Lenox Square (3393 Peachtree Road NE, 404-261-2663) and Park Place Shopping Center (4505 Ashford-Dunwoody Road, 404-396-1800). © 1988 Polo/Ralph Lauren Corporation.

The mail-order form being intact is a nice bonus for collectors — it gives a complete picture of how customers actually engaged with this catalog at the time.
