Polo Ralph Lauren — 1989 Equestrian Edition. Official in-store catalog for The Polo Ralph Lauren Atlanta Shop at Lenox Square and Park Place Shopping Center.

**Cover:** Dramatic equestrian hunt scene — riders on horseback rendered in a dark, oil-painting aesthetic. Gold "POLO RALPH LAUREN" logotype. Visually one of the most striking covers in the Atlanta Shop series.
