## Brief summary

This is a genuine copy of Esquire's Wear & Care Guide, Volume 1, Number 1,
published by Esquire, Inc. (488 Madison Avenue, New York). It is a special
newsstand-only men's fashion and grooming guide, not a regular monthly Esquire
issue — the contents page explicitly states "SOLD ONLY AT NEWSDEALERS." The
publication covers wardrobe coordination, pattern mixing, grooming, and
includes a stain removal encyclopedia and traveler's almanac. Cover price was
$2.50. Condition is Fair overall — covers show significant wear and soiling,
