─── RESEARCH NOTES — Item 1 ───

Authenticity & fakes:
  Modern reproductions of cast iron sad irons exist in country-decor
  retail (Cracker Barrel, Hobby Lobby, etc.) but are generally
  visually distinguishable from authentic vintage by:
  - Surface finish: modern repros are typically uniformly painted
    matte black; authentic vintage has natural sand-cast texture with
    organic patina variation.
  - Soleplate: modern repros have smooth machined soleplates;
    authentic examples show period use-wear (scratches, polish
    burnishing, occasional pitting).
  - Handle craftsmanship: modern fluted handles are die-cast and
    uniform; authentic wrought-iron twisted handles show slight
    asymmetry and hand-finishing marks at attachment points.
  Photos 1–4 collectively confirm authentic vintage attribution for
  this iron. The visible "5" on the body is a weight designation, not
  a forgery concern.

Listing restrictions & legal issues:
  No restrictions. Standard antique household item. Ships
  domestically and internationally without issue.
  Iron and steel content: no hazmat or shipping concerns.

Known scams & buyer issues:
  - "Hidden crack not disclosed" returns occasionally happen with
    cast-iron items. Photograph all sides + soleplate under bright
    light pre-listing. A bright pinging sound when tapped = sound;
    dull thud = hidden crack.
  - "Reproduction sold as antique" disputes — mitigated by the strong
    multi-angle photography already on hand. Lead the listing with
    the natural patina, sand-cast texture, and soleplate use-wear
    photos as authenticity evidence.

Authentication services available:
  N/A through eBay. Specialized sad iron collector communities exist
  (Pressing Iron and Trivet Collectors of America — PITCA) for
  attribution questions on high-value pieces.

Disclosure recommendations:
  - Total weight (photograph on a kitchen scale for confirmation —
    the "5" embossed should match; if it does, mention it in the
    title as "Size 5" — collectors search for specific sizes).
  - Exact dimensions (length × width × height).
  - Maker stamp photographed if present, or explicit "unmarked" note.
  - Soleplate condition: photograph clearly; disclose scratches /
    polish wear / pitting / any rust pitting if present.
  - Handle condition: any hairline cracks at attachment points,
    losses to spiral fluting, any repairs.
  - Body condition: hairline cracks (rare but valuable to confirm
    absent), any chips at heel or point.
  - Sound test if possible: confirm "rings true" rather than dull
    thud (indicates no hidden cracks).
  - "Decorative use, not tested for hot ironing" — standard
    disclosure even for clearly functional-looking examples.
