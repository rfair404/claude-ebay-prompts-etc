Polo Ralph Lauren "On Safari" — one of the most collectible pieces in the Ralph Lauren ephemera world.

Zebra-print burlap fabric cover with cream label bearing the cursive "On Safari" title and the Polo Ralph Lauren polo player mark. The Polo Ralph Lauren Shop (Atlanta) attribution on the spine.

The editorial inside reads like a private travel diary — named models on what appears to be an actual African safari: Jane, Justin, Clotilde, Peter. Handwritten captions accompany the photographs. Interior spreads include a lion cub being held by two models in safari dress, a model on horseback beside a zebra (caption: "Jane got on it, and we were all struck by how beautiful she was"), and wide savanna landscape shots. Fashion as pure adventure storytelling.

This is early Polo Ralph Lauren at its most cinematic. Solo comp sales of this specific title regularly reach $300.
