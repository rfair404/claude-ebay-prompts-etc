## DESCRIPTION (paste as-is into eBay)

Polo Ralph Lauren × Muniá Atlanta — Volume II, Fall 1977. Early-era Polo catalog from one of Atlanta's most distinctive specialty retailers. One of the earliest known volumes in the Muniá Atlanta series — predating Vol IV by four years.

**Cover:** White/cream cover with partial landscape edge. Back cover carries "VOLUME II / FALL 1977" identifier.

**Interior:** Opens with a full Ralph Lauren portrait spread — RL in denim, relaxed and confident — paired with the statement of his design philosophy: "Fashion changes with every whim. Style is permanent, enduring. 'Style, not fashion' is what Ralph Lauren stands for. He believes in tradition, quality and classic design. In clothing that becomes even better and more personal with age."

This is Polo Ralph Lauren in 1977 — years before the brand's cultural dominance — documented through a regional Atlanta specialty retailer that no longer exists. A genuine piece of Southern fashion history.
