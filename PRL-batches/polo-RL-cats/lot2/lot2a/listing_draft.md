Polo Ralph Lauren × Muniá Atlanta — Vol IV, Fall 1981. A store-branded catalog from one of Atlanta's most distinctive specialty retailers, long since closed. This isn't just a Polo catalog — it's a piece of Atlanta fashion history.

**Cover:** Dynamic action shot of three mounted polo players mid-match. "Polo Ralph Lauren" logotype on the left, "Muniá ATLANTA" branding on the right vertical strip.

**Back cover:** Dark sage green. "Vol IV Fall 1981" + polo player logo + "We believe in style, not fashion."

**Interior:** Two full editorial threads — a menswear section (tweed sport coats, striped Shetland sweaters, lifestyle collage including Royal Palm Polo Club signage and vintage Jeep scenes) and a women's western/Southwestern section (concho belts, Navajo-pattern knitwear, denim).
